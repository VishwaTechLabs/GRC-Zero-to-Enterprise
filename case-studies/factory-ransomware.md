# Case Study — Factory Ransomware

## Scenario
Manufacturing site experiences ransomware affecting operational systems. Assess OT asset criticality, segmentation, remote access, incident governance, recovery and safety implications.

## Student tasks
1. Define scope.
2. Identify assets/processes.
3. Identify risks.
4. Select controls.
5. Define evidence.
6. Assign owners.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Prepare executive summary.
9. Identify framework mappings.
10. Defend the recommendation in a viva.
