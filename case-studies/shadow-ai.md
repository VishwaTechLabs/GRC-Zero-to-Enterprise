# Case Study — Shadow Ai

## Scenario
Employees use public GenAI tools with company information. Build AI inventory, risk assessment, policy, controls, awareness and monitoring.

## Student tasks
1. Define scope.
2. Identify assets/processes.
3. Identify risks.
4. Select controls.
5. Define evidence.
6. Assign owners.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Prepare executive summary.
9. Identify framework mappings.
10. Defend the recommendation in a viva.
