# Case Study — Saas Vendor Breach

## Scenario
Critical SaaS vendor reports unauthorized access to customer data. Assess contract, evidence, incident governance, privacy escalation, risk and remediation.

## Student tasks
1. Define scope.
2. Identify assets/processes.
3. Identify risks.
4. Select controls.
5. Define evidence.
6. Assign owners.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Prepare executive summary.
9. Identify framework mappings.
10. Defend the recommendation in a viva.
