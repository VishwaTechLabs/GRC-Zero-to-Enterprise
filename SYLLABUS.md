# 📚 COMPLETE 30-DAY GRC ZERO-TO-HERO SYLLABUS

## 🟦 Foundations
**Days 1–2:** GRC, cybersecurity, governance, operating model, Three Lines and RACI.

## 🟩 Risk
**Days 3–6:** enterprise/cyber/technology/operational/strategic/regulatory/vendor risk,
asset/threat context, scoring, quantification, appetite, tolerance, treatment and acceptance.

## 🟨 Controls & Assurance
**Days 7–9:** control taxonomy, design/operating effectiveness, evidence, testing,
policies, standards, procedures and exceptions.

## 🟪 Frameworks & Compliance
**Days 10–14:** ISO 27001/27002/27005, NIST CSF/RMF/800-53/800-61, CIS, COBIT, ITIL,
SOC 2, PCI DSS, privacy and data governance.

## 🟥 Audit & Enterprise Risk
**Days 15–16:** internal audit, CAPA, findings, root cause, third-party/vendor risk and due diligence.

## ☁️ Cybersecurity GRC
**Days 17–20:** cloud, IAM/PAM/IGA, SOC, incident response, vulnerability management,
AppSec, DevSecOps, software supply chain, SBOM and provenance.

## 🔄 Resilience & Reporting
**Days 21–22:** BCM, DR, BIA, RTO/RPO, KRI, KPI, KCI, dashboards and executive reporting.

## 🤖🏭 Specialized GRC
**Days 23–24:** AI governance, AI risk, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, GenAI, AI supply chain,
OT/ICS, SCADA, PLC, DCS, Purdue, IEC 62443 and NIST 800-82.

## 🌐 Regulatory & Technology
**Days 25–27:** regulatory obligation management, India-focused context, GRC platforms,
workflow, automation and enterprise implementation.

## 🏆 Capstone
**Days 28–30:** risk/control build, mock audit, executive simulation, final presentation,
viva and career portfolio.

### Final learner capability

**Business objective → Risk → Control → Evidence → Test → Finding → Remediation →
Residual Risk → Metric → Executive Decision.**
