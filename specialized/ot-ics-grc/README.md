# OT/ICS GRC — Dedicated Module

## Core concepts

IT and OT have different operational priorities. OT environments can include PLCs, SCADA,
DCS, HMI, RTUs, engineering workstations, industrial networks and safety systems.

## GRC focus

- Asset inventory
- Criticality
- Safety and security relationship
- Availability
- Change management
- Remote/vendor access
- Segmentation
- Backup/recovery
- Vulnerability management
- Monitoring
- Incident response
- Third-party maintenance
- Business continuity

## Purdue-style teaching model

Enterprise → Industrial DMZ → Operations → Control → Field devices

Use the model to discuss segmentation, trust boundaries, access paths and evidence.

## OT risk register fields

`OT Risk ID | Asset | Process | Safety Impact | Availability Impact | Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Control | Owner | Evidence`
