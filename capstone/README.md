# Enterprise GRC Capstone

## Company
VishwaTech Bank: 2,000 employees, customer web/mobile systems, AWS workloads,
corporate IAM, customer PII, payment processing, SOC, software teams and 15 critical vendors.

## Required deliverables

### Governance
- GRC charter
- Operating model
- RACI
- Committee cadence

### Risk
- Asset inventory
- Risk methodology
- 25+ risks
- Heatmap
- Treatment plan
- Risk acceptance

### Controls
- 40+ controls
- Owners
- Evidence
- Test procedures
- Framework mappings

### Compliance
Map controls to selected ISO, NIST, CIS, SOC 2, PCI DSS and privacy requirements.

### Enterprise security
Include IAM/PAM/IGA, cloud, SOC, AppSec, DevSecOps, data and BCM.

### AI
- AI inventory
- AI risk assessment
- AI control library
- AI vendor assessment
- AI incident scenario

### OT/ICS
- OT asset inventory
- Purdue-style segmentation review
- OT risk register
- Vendor remote-access assessment
- OT incident scenario

### Audit
- Audit plan
- Evidence index
- 10 control tests
- Findings
- Root cause
- CAPA
- Management response
- Closure criteria

### Executive
- Risk dashboard
- KRI/KPI/KCI
- Top risks
- Overdue actions
- Control effectiveness
- Vendor risk
- AI risk
- OT risk
- Board decisions

### Final presentation
12–15 slides and 20-minute viva.
