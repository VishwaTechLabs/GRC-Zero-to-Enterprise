# Enterprise Information Security Policy

## Purpose
Define management expectations for protecting information and technology.

## Scope
People, processes, applications, infrastructure, data and relevant third parties.

## Mandatory statements
- Access follows least privilege.
- Sensitive data is protected according to classification.
- Security incidents are reported promptly.
- Security controls are periodically reviewed.
- Exceptions require documented risk and approval.
- Evidence must be retained according to approved requirements.

## Roles
Board/Executive, CISO, GRC, IT, Engineering, HR, Legal, Procurement and business owners.

## Exceptions
Record business justification, risk, compensating controls, approver and expiry.

## Review
Define owner, approval authority and review frequency.
