# Day 24 — OT/ICS GRC

## Learning objective
Teach IT vs OT, ICS, SCADA, PLC, DCS, HMI, RTU, Purdue architecture, safety/security, availability, segmentation, remote access and industrial incident governance.

## Why this matters
A GRC professional must translate technical and business conditions into measurable
risk, accountable controls and defensible evidence. This lesson develops that skill.

## Concepts to teach
- Business objectives and scope
- Stakeholders and ownership
- Risk and control relationships
- Evidence and assurance
- Exceptions and remediation
- Management reporting
- Auditability and traceability

## Instructor flow
1. Explain the concept in plain business language.
2. Show a realistic enterprise scenario.
3. Draw the process from business objective to evidence.
4. Demonstrate a sample artifact.
5. Let students perform the lab.
6. Review mistakes and explain audit implications.
7. Ask scenario-based interview questions.

## Enterprise scenario
VishwaTech Bank operates web/mobile applications, cloud workloads, corporate identity,
customer data, security operations and third-party services. Students act as GRC
analysts supporting the CISO.

## Practical lab
**Assess an OT environment and create a risk register.**

### Required output
1. Scope
2. Current state
3. Risks/gaps
4. Controls/actions
5. Owner
6. Evidence
7. Priority
8. Management recommendation

## Evidence examples
Approved policy, system report, ticket, configuration record, access-review export,
audit log, training record, meeting minutes, contract clause, test result, exception
approval or signed management response.

## Interview questions
- What is the business risk?
- Who owns the risk?
- Who owns the control?
- What evidence proves operation?
- How would you test it?
- What happens if the control fails?
- How would you report it to an executive?
- How would you map it to a framework?

## Homework
Produce a one-page management summary plus the lab artifact.

## Instructor checkpoint
The learner must be able to explain:
**risk → control → owner → evidence → test → finding → remediation → residual risk.**
