# Instructor Teaching Guide

## Daily format — 3 hours recommended
- 15 min recap
- 40 min theory
- 30 min enterprise architecture/case
- 60 min lab
- 20 min learner presentation
- 10 min interview questions
- 5 min homework

For a 6-hour class, expand with a second lab, group exercise, framework mapping and viva.

## Teaching rule
Do not teach frameworks as memorization. Teach:
business objective → obligation → risk → control → evidence → test → finding → remediation.

## Assessment
- Daily labs: 25%
- Framework/control mapping: 15%
- Mock audit: 15%
- Capstone: 30%
- Viva/interview: 15%

## Passing
Recommended: 70% overall and successful completion of the capstone.

## Instructor checklist
- [ ] Explain business impact.
- [ ] Assign ownership.
- [ ] Require evidence.
- [ ] Show control testing.
- [ ] Discuss residual risk.
- [ ] Connect to executive reporting.
- [ ] Avoid unsupported legal/compliance claims.
